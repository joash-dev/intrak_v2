Student Documents
Review, approve, reject, or request resubmission of documents from your assigned students.
/instructor/documents
Reviewing a document
1
Open a pending document submission from a student
2
Review the uploaded file or form submission
3
Choose: Approve, Reject, or Request Resubmission
4
Add a remark (required for Reject and Resubmission)
5
Submit your decision — student is notified automatically
Document statuses
PENDING— awaiting your review
APPROVED
REJECTED
RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED
Always include clear, actionable remarks when rejecting or requesting resubmission so students know exactly what to correct.

Reviewing an application
1

2

3

Module Guide
Company Proposals
Review non-listed company proposals submitted by students. You are the first reviewer before the coordinator.
/instructor/company-proposals
Your 3 possible actions
Return for Correction
Send the proposal back to the student with remarks on what needs to be fixed.
Reject
Reject the proposal outright if the company is not suitable.
Forward to Coordinator
Approve the proposal for coordinator-level final review and decision.
Proposal flow after your action
1
Student submits → SUBMITTED_TO_INSTRUCTOR
2
You return → RETURNED_BY_INSTRUCTOR
3
You reject → REJECTED_BY_INSTRUCTOR
4
You forward → FORWARDED_TO_COORDINATOR

Troubleshooting Guide
Common instructor issues and how to resolve them.
!
I can't see a student's documents
Confirm the student is assigned to you under Students
Check with your coordinator if the assignment is missing
!
A module or feature is not visible
Ensure you are logged in with the Instructor role
Some features are condition-based — check workflow status
!
I can't forward a proposal
Verify the proposal is in SUBMITTED_TO_INSTRUCTOR status
Ensure you have added any required remarks before forwarding
Escalation path
1
Try the troubleshooting steps above
2
Escalate to your Coordinator
3
Coordinator escalates to Admin / Technical Team
