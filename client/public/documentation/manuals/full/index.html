INTRAK User Manual

INTRAK is the Internship and OJT Tracking and Management System. This manual covers every feature, workflow, and setting available to all five user roles — students, instructors, coordinators, industry partners, and administrators.

What INTRAK Does

Documents
Submit, review, and track required internship documents by phase.
Company Placement
Browse, apply, and track student placement at partner companies.
Attendance
Log and verify daily attendance with total hours tracking.
Evaluations
Partners submit evaluations; students view their performance results.
Reports
Compliance and operational reporting at instructor and coordinator level.
Notifications
Role-based alerts for all workflow actions and announcements.
Authentication

Access & Login

All users access INTRAK through the same login page. The system automatically routes you to the correct portal based on your assigned role.

Login Process

1
Open the official INTRAK system URL in your browser
2
Enter your registered email address and password
3
If Two-Factor Authentication is enabled, enter the one-time code sent to your email, or use a backup code
4
The system redirects you to your role-specific dashboard automatically

Public Authentication Routes

/login
/forgot-password
/reset-password
/verify-email

Password Recovery

1
Go to /forgot-password and submit your registered email
2
Check your inbox (including spam folder) for the reset link
3
Follow the link to /reset-password and set a new password
If you do not receive the reset email after a few minutes, check your spam folder, then contact your instructor or admin.
Interface

Navigation & Interface

All role portals share the same layout pattern — a sidebar navigation, header shortcuts, and a responsive main content area.

Common Interface Elements

Sidebar Navigation

Header
Notifications icon with unread indicator, profile/settings access, and logout.
Notification Panel
Slide-out panel with all system and workflow alerts. Click to jump to related module.

Core Workflows

1 — Document Compliance Workflow

The document workflow governs all required submissions from students through the review and approval process.

1
Student uploads or generates required document via Documents module
Student
2
Instructor or Coordinator reviews the submission
Instructor / Coordinator
3
Reviewer approves, rejects, or requests resubmission with written remarks
Reviewer
4
Student receives notification, reads remarks, corrects and resubmits if needed
Student
5
Compliance status reflected in dashboards and reports for all reviewers
All

2 — Company Placement Workflow

The placement workflow handles how students apply to and get assigned to partner companies.

1
Student browses available partner companies and submits a placement application
Student
2
Instructor or Coordinator reviews the application and student eligibility
Instructor / Coordinator
3
Application status updated to Approved, Rejected, or Withdrawn
Reviewer
4
Once assigned, Attendance and Evaluation modules become fully usable for the student
Student
If a student's preferred company is not listed, they can propose it via Partnership Assistance before applying.

3 — Partnership Proposal Workflow

Students can propose non-listed companies through a structured two-stage review process.

1
Student submits a non-listed company proposal with supporting attachments
Student
2
Instructor reviews and either returns for correction, rejects, or forwards to coordinator
Instructor
3
Coordinator performs final review and updates the proposal to a terminal status
Coordinator

Proposal Status Flow

SUBMITTED_TO_INSTRUCTOR— student submitted, awaiting instructor
RETURNED_BY_INSTRUCTOR— sent back for correction
REJECTED_BY_INSTRUCTOR
FORWARDED_TO_COORDINATOR
UNDER_COORDINATOR_REVIEW
PENDING_EXTERNAL_APPROVAL
APPROVED
REJECTED

4 — Attendance & Evaluation Workflow

After placement, students log attendance and industry partners verify hours and submit evaluations.

1
Student logs attendance entry (manual entry or QR-supported method)
Student
2
Industry Partner reviews and verifies or rejects the attendance entry
Industry Partner
3
Industry Partner submits a structured performance evaluation with ratings and comments
Industry Partner
4
Student views evaluation results in their Evaluations module
Student
Attendance and Evaluation modules are only accessible after a student has an approved placement with a company and supervisor assigned.
Portal Guide

Student Portal

Student
Submit documents, apply to companies, log attendance, and view your evaluations.
Base path: /student · 9 modules available

The Student Portal is your primary workspace throughout the internship program. Every document you submit, every application you make, and every attendance entry you log is managed here.

Dashboard

Your dashboard gives you an instant overview of where you are in the internship program. Path: /student/dashboard

Progress Summary
See your current internship stage and overall completion indicators.
Document Status
Quick view of pending, approved, and rejected documents needing action.
Company Context
Your assigned company and supervisor once placement is confirmed.
Announcements
Latest announcements published by your coordinator.
Check your dashboard at the start of every session. Pending document actions and new announcements appear here first.

Documents

The Documents module is where you submit all required internship requirements. Path: /student/documents

Submitting a Document

1
Go to /student/documents and view your requirement checklist by phase
2
Click the document item you need to submit
3
Upload a file (verify type and size) or fill out the structured form if required
4
Submit — status changes to PENDING and your reviewer is notified

Document Statuses

PENDING— submitted, awaiting review
APPROVED— accepted, no action needed
REJECTED— read remarks and correct
RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED— correction required

Dynamic Document Forms

Some documents require filling an online form instead of uploading a file. Navigate to /student/documents/form/:type, fill in all fields, preview the generated document, then submit directly into the review workflow.

Templates

Path: /student/templates
Download official document templates published by authorized personnel. Always use the latest template version — using outdated templates may result in document rejection. Download the template, fill it in, then upload the completed file under Documents.

Companies

Browse and apply to available partner companies for your internship placement. Path: /student/companies

1
Browse available partner companies and review details and available slots
2
Click Apply on your preferred company and confirm
3
Monitor your application status from the same page

Application Statuses

PENDING— awaiting review
APPROVED— placement confirmed
REJECTED
WITHDRAWN— cancelled by you
If your preferred company is not listed, use Partnership Assistance to propose it before applying.

Partnership Assistance

Propose a company that is not yet listed as a partner. Your proposal will go through instructor and coordinator review. Path: /student/partnership-assistance

1
Fill in the company proposal details and upload supporting documents (e.g., company profile, MOA)
2
Submit — status becomes SUBMITTED_TO_INSTRUCTOR
3
Monitor progress in the proposal timeline and respond to instructor messages
4
Once approved by coordinator, the company may be added to the partner list

Attendance

Log your daily internship attendance and track your total verified hours. Path: /student/attendance

This module is only available after you have been assigned to a company and supervisor. Check your application status if attendance is unavailable.
1
Log your attendance entry for the day (manual or QR-supported method)
2
Entry status becomes PENDING — your industry partner will verify it
3
Once verified and approved, hours are counted toward your total

You can view your full attendance log, total completed hours, verification status per entry, and export attendance records where available.

Evaluations

View the performance evaluation submitted by your industry partner supervisor. Path: /student/evaluations
You will receive a notification when a new evaluation is submitted. Evaluations include criteria ratings and qualitative comments. You cannot edit evaluations — contact your instructor or coordinator if you have concerns.

Portal Guide

Instructor Portal

Instructor
Review documents, manage applications and proposals, and monitor your assigned students.
Base path: /instructor · 7 modules available

As an instructor, you are the first reviewer in the document compliance and company proposal workflows. You are responsible for your assigned students' progress and placement readiness.

Student Documents

Path: /instructor/documents
Review all document submissions from your assigned students. For every submission you can Approve, Reject, or Request Resubmission. Always include clear, actionable remarks when rejecting or requesting resubmission so students know exactly what to correct.

1
Open a PENDING document submission from your student list
2
Review the uploaded file or form content
3
Choose Approve, Reject, or Request Resubmission and add a remark
4
Submit your decision — the student is notified automatically

Company Applications

Path: /instructor/applications
Review company placement applications submitted by your assigned students. Approve or reject based on eligibility and pre-deployment document completion. Once approved, the student gains access to Attendance and Evaluation modules.

Before approving a placement, ensure the student has completed all required pre-deployment documents.

Company Proposals

Path: /instructor/company-proposals
You are the first reviewer for non-listed company proposals. You have three actions available:

Return for Correction
Send back to the student with remarks. Status becomes RETURNED_BY_INSTRUCTOR.
Reject
Reject the proposal outright. Status becomes REJECTED_BY_INSTRUCTOR.
Forward to Coordinator
Approve for coordinator final review. Status becomes FORWARDED_TO_COORDINATOR.

Students

Path: /instructor/students
View profiles, activity timelines, document completion rates, and placement information for all students assigned to you. Use this view to monitor progress and identify students who need follow-up.

Reports

Path: /instructor/reports
Generate monitoring and compliance reports for your assigned students. Available reports include attendance summaries, document completion rates, and placement status overviews. Export is available where enabled in the UI.

Portal Guide

Coordinator Portal

Coordinator
Program-wide oversight — students, companies, proposals, announcements, and reporting.
Base path: /coordinator · 8 modules available

Coordinators have the highest level of program oversight. You make final decisions on proposals, manage the partner company list, publish announcements, and generate program-wide reports.

Students

Path: /coordinator/students
Monitor all students enrolled in the program — not limited to a single instructor's assignment. You can assign or reassign instructors to students, track document compliance, and identify students needing intervention.

Companies

Path: /coordinator/companies
Manage the partner company records visible to students for placement applications. Track internship slot capacity and current assignments per company. Add new companies or update existing records as needed.

Company Proposals

Path: /coordinator/company-proposals
You make the final decision on all company proposals forwarded by instructors. Proposals appear in your queue only after an instructor has reviewed and forwarded them.

Approve
Accept the company. May be added to the partner list for future student applications.
Pending External Approval
Mark as awaiting additional institutional or external sign-off before final decision.
Reject
Reject with remarks explaining the decision to the student and instructor.

Announcements

Path: /coordinator/announcements
Create, publish, and manage program announcements targeted to specific user groups. Pinned announcements appear prominently on student and instructor dashboards.

1
Click Create and write the announcement title and body
2
Select the target audience (all users, students only, instructors, etc.)
3
Optionally pin the announcement for prominent display
4
Publish — recipients receive notifications immediately

Reports

Path: /coordinator/reports
Generate program-wide operational reports including total placements, pending document counts, compliance rates, and aggregate attendance data. Export to available formats for institutional submission and administration.

Portal Guide

Industry Partner Portal

Industry Partner
Verify intern attendance and submit structured performance evaluations.
Base path: /industry-partner · 5 modules available

As an industry partner, you supervise interns from the institution at your company. Your two primary responsibilities are verifying their daily attendance and submitting performance evaluations.

Interns are assigned to you by the institution's coordinator. You will only see the interns under your direct supervision. Contact the coordinator if an expected intern does not appear in your portal.

Attendance Verification

Path: /industry-partner/attendance
Review and verify daily attendance entries submitted by your interns. Only approved entries count toward the intern's total completed hours.

1
Open a PENDING attendance entry and review the date, time, and intern details
2
Approve if the entry is accurate, or Reject with a remark explaining why it is invalid
3
Submit your verification — the intern is notified of the result
Please verify attendance entries promptly and accurately. Delayed verification prevents interns from tracking their progress.

Evaluations

Path: /industry-partner/evaluations
Submit structured performance evaluations for your assigned interns. Evaluations are visible to the intern and their instructor once submitted.

1
Go to Evaluations and select the intern to evaluate
2
Fill in ratings for each defined performance criterion
3
Add qualitative comments — be specific, professional, and constructive
4
Submit — the intern and instructor are notified immediately
Your comments are the most valuable part of the evaluation for the intern's development. Be specific about both strengths and areas for improvement.
Portal Guide

Admin Portal

Admin
Platform-level controls: users, companies, system settings, and maintenance mode.
Base path: /admin · 5 modules available

Administrators manage the platform infrastructure. Admin actions often affect all users — proceed carefully, especially for user deletion and maintenance mode.

Many admin actions are irreversible. Deleting users may remove associated records. Enabling maintenance mode immediately locks out all non-admin users.

User Management

Path: /admin/users
Create, update, deactivate, and remove user accounts for all five roles. When creating a user, assign the appropriate role — the user will be redirected to the correct portal upon login.

Prefer deactivating over deleting users. Deactivation suspends access without removing any associated data or records.

Roles you can create

Student — /student Instructor — /instructor Coordinator — /coordinator Industry Partner — /industry-partner Admin — /admin

Company Management

Path: /admin/companies
Maintain the master company database. Companies added here become available for student placement applications. Ensure all company details are accurate before saving — students rely on this information when choosing where to apply.

Admin Settings

Path: /admin/settings

System Configuration
Platform-level settings affecting all users and modules
Maintenance Mode
When enabled, non-admin users see a maintenance page — disable promptly after work
Export Settings
Export current admin configuration to a backup file
Import Settings
Restore or apply a previously exported configuration file
NAS / Storage Options
Manage infrastructure storage options where configured

Account Security

Security & Account Settings

INTRAK uses a multi-layer security model to protect user accounts and ensure role-based access control.

Role-based Route Protection
All portal routes are guarded — unauthorized users are redirected to login. Access and refresh token session model.
Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Enable/disable from account settings. Login challenge with one-time email code. Backup code generation and regeneration supported.
Email Verification
Verify your account email via token workflow. Check and manage verification status from account settings.
Profile Photo Management
Upload, replace, or remove your photo. Changes reflect across all portal header and profile panels.
