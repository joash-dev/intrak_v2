Your internship journey
Follow these major phases in order to complete your OJT successfully.
1
Log in and complete your profile under Settings
2
Submit required pre-deployment documents under Documents
3
Browse companies and submit your placement application
4
Wait for application approval from your instructor or coordinator
5
Begin logging attendance once assigned to a company and supervisor
6
Submit remaining required documents throughout the internship
7
View your performance evaluation submitted by your industry partner
Attendance and evaluation modules only become available after you are assigned to a company and supervisor.
Authentication
Login & Access
How to log in, recover your password, and verify your email.
How to log in
1
Open the official INTRAK system URL
2
Enter your registered email and password
3
If 2FA is enabled, enter the one-time code sent to your email or use a backup code
4
You will be redirected to your Student Dashboard
Forgot your password?
1
Go to /forgot-password
2
Enter your registered email address and submit
3
Check your inbox for the reset link
4
Click the link and set a new password on /reset-password
If you do not receive the reset email, check your spam/junk folder and try resending. Contact your instructor if the issue persists.

Module Guide
Documents & Requirements
Upload, track, and resubmit your internship documents through the review cycle.
/student/documents
Submitting a document
1
Go to /student/documents and view your requirement checklist
2
Click the document you need to submit and choose upload file or fill form
3
Attach your file (verify type and size meet policy) and submit
4
Status changes to PENDING — your reviewer has been notified
Document statuses explained
PENDING— submitted, awaiting review
APPROVED— accepted, no action needed
REJECTED— not accepted, read the remarks
RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED— correct and resubmit
Resubmitting a document
1
Open the document with REJECTED or RESUBMISSION_REQUESTED status
2
Read the reviewer's remarks carefully
3
Correct your document based on the feedback
4
Upload the corrected version and resubmit
Dynamic Document Forms
Some documents require filling out a structured online form instead of uploading a file.
1
Navigate to /student/documents/form/:type for form-based documents
2
Fill in all required fields in the structured form
3
Preview the generated document before finalizing
4
Submit directly — it enters the same review workflow

Module Guide
Logging Attendance
Record your daily internship hours and track verification by your industry partner supervisor.
/student/attendance
Attendance is only available after you have been assigned to a company and supervisor. If the module is unavailable, check your application status.
Logging an attendance entry
1
Go to /student/attendance
2
Log your attendance entry for the day (manual or QR-supported method)
3
Entry status becomes PENDING — your industry partner will verify it
4
Once verified, hours are counted toward your total completed hours
What you can view
Attendance Logs
Full list of all your logged entries with dates, times, and verification status.
Total Hours
Running total of verified hours completed so far.
Verification Status
Whether each entry has been approved, rejected, or is still pending.
Export
Export your attendance records where available in the UI.

Help
Troubleshooting Guide
Common issues students encounter and how to resolve them.
!
I can't log in
Double-check your email and password
Use /forgot-password to reset your password
Check if your account is still active — ask your instructor
!
I didn't receive a 2FA or verification code
Check your spam or junk folder
Wait a moment and use the resend option
Confirm your registered email is correct under Settings
!
My document upload is failing
Verify the file type and size meet policy requirements
Check if this document type requires a form submission instead
Try on a stable internet connection
!
Attendance module is not available
You must have an assigned company and supervisor first
Your application must be APPROVED
Ask your coordinator or instructor to check your assignment state
!
A feature or module is not visible
Some modules require specific workflow conditions to be met
Make sure you're logged in as a Student role
Contact your instructor if the issue persists
Escalation path
If you can't resolve an issue, follow this order:
1
Try the troubleshooting steps above first
2
Contact your assigned Instructor
3
Escalate to the Coordinator if unresolved
4
Coordinator escalates to the Admin / Technical Team
When reporting an issue, include: your full name, screenshot of the error, date/time, page URL, and what you expected to happen vs what occurred.
