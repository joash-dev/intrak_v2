Module Guide
Attendance Verification
Review and verify daily attendance entries submitted by your interns.
/industry-partner/attendance
How to verify an attendance entry
1
Go to /industry-partner/attendance
2
Review the pending attendance entry details (intern, date, time)
3
Choose: Approve or Reject
4
Add a remark if rejecting — explain why the entry is invalid
5
Submit your verification — the intern is notified
Only approved entries count toward the intern's total completed hours. Please verify entries promptly and accurately.
Verification workflow
PENDING— intern submitted, waiting for you
APPROVED— hours counted
REJECTED— not counted, intern notified
Module Guide
Intern Evaluations
Submit structured performance evaluations for your assigned interns.
/industry-partner/evaluations
Submitting an evaluation
1
Go to /industry-partner/evaluations
2
Select the intern you want to evaluate
3
Fill in ratings for each performance criteria
4
Add comments — be specific and constructive
5
Submit — the intern and their instructor are notified
Evaluation comments are visible to the intern. Be professional, specific, and constructive in your feedback.
What evaluations include
Criteria Ratings
Scale-based ratings across defined performance categories set by the institution.
Comments
Open-text comments providing qualitative feedback to the intern.

Troubleshooting Guide
Common industry partner issues and how to resolve them.
!
I can't see any interns
Interns are assigned to you by the institution's coordinator
Contact the program coordinator if no interns appear under your account
!
I can't find an attendance entry
The intern may not have submitted an entry for that date yet
Check with the intern directly and ask them to log the entry
!
I can't submit an evaluation
Verify you are selecting the correct intern
Ensure all required criteria fields are filled in before submitting
Contact the coordinator if access is blocked
For system issues, contact the institution's program coordinator. They will escalate to the Admin or technical team on your behalf.
